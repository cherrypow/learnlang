Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

ThaiLang, JapanLang, ManLang, KoreanLang, RusLang, HebrewLang, HindiLang and ArabLang do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Each app runs entirely on your device, with no server communication.

Data collection: None. No personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers.

Third-party services: None. No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Local storage: Your progress and settings are saved on your device and never leave it.

Children's privacy: The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.
